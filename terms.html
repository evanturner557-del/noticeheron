About this website

This website describes Noticeheron: a plain-English weekly email listing new planning applications in your area, each linked to the council's own record. Nothing can be bought or paid for on this website. Sending an enquiry does not create a contract.

Anything described as draft or planned is not yet available. Prices shown as planned may change before launch.

Prices

Free during the draft stage; a paid tier may follow. Any work or purchase would be agreed separately by email, with the price and what is included confirmed in writing first, and your statutory rights as a consumer apply.

What we don't do

Noticeheron is independent and not affiliated with any council. The council's planning register is the official record.
